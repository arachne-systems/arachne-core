### Admission fixture correction

The requester had cancelled its local task. That does not prove that the
owner has received QUIC STOP and marked its held exchange as expired.
`ControlRequest::respond` queues an in-process reply; it does not confirm
remote receipt. The test could therefore adopt while the exchange was still
open on the owner, then incorrectly require a push.

Thirty unchanged exact repeats and three unchanged full 13-test binary runs
passed. Their receipts are `/tmp/arachne-h7-push-unchanged-repeats/receipt.json`
and `/tmp/arachne-h7-push-whole-repeats/receipt.json`. These passes do not erase
the original failure.

The fixture now reads the owner's observed expired-waiter count through the
existing `test-fixtures` harness. It must see one expired exchange before it
stages and adopts the admission. The existing deadline stays unchanged. The
test also requires zero replies on the old exchange and exactly one queued
push. No fixed delay or production delivery change was added. A push remains
one bounded attempt; the durable retained result supports requester retry.

Commit `911ecce` contains this fixture change. Fifty corrected exact repeats
and the complete 13-test binary passed. The receipt is
`/tmp/arachne-h7-push-green/receipt.json`. The format check also passed.
