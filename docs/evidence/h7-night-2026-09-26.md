## Suspend lock regression

Strict Clippy found that `Node::suspend` dropped a borrowed map instead of its
mutex guard. The guard stayed live across `close_idle` and `pause_mdns` awaits.
A new test requires the suspend/resume futures to be `Send`, then executes
both operations and checks the suspended state.

The first compile failed with E0277: the parked-map `std::sync::MutexGuard`
could not move between threads. The receipt is
`/tmp/arachne-h7-suspend-red.jsonl`. A lexical scope now releases that guard
before either await. The compile and execution then passed: one test, zero
failed in 0.01 seconds. Receipts: `/tmp/arachne-h7-suspend-green.jsonl` and
`/tmp/arachne-h7-suspend-green-test.log`.
